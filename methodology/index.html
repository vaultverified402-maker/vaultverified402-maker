What the record means.

This page defines the public terms and standards used by Vault Verified. It describes what users may rely on when reading a Vault record without disclosing proprietary collection, detection, scoring, automation, or operational systems.

Neutrality

Vault Verified is an independent record-preservation and verification platform. Vault Verified does not operate a sportsbook, accept wagers, place bets, set odds, handle betting funds, or guarantee outcomes.

Verification is not endorsement.

A status, record, grade, or designation displayed by Vault Verified is not a recommendation, guarantee of future performance, certification of expertise, or endorsement of any operator, service, selection, or claim.

Defined terms

INDEPENDENT

Vault's preservation and grading of a record are maintained separately from the person or organization whose activity is recorded. The subject cannot retroactively alter the original preserved evidence or its historical capture time.

VERIFIED OPERATOR

A claimed operator identity that has satisfied Vault Verified's applicable verification requirements. Verified Operator status concerns identity and participation in the Vault standard; it is not an endorsement or guarantee of performance.

TRACKED / UNCLAIMED

A consultant identity for which Vault has independently collected qualifying publicly observable information and whose Vault profile has not been claimed by that consultant.

OBSERVED

Information captured by Vault from an eligible publicly accessible source under the methodology in force at the time of observation.

PRESERVED

Evidence retained with its provenance and capture time so the historical record cannot be silently rewritten.

IMMUTABLE

The original preserved evidence is not silently altered. Later corrections, interpretations, grading changes, disputes, and status changes are recorded separately rather than rewriting the original evidence.

GRADED

An eligible selection whose outcome has been determined against the applicable final event result.

AUDIT

A defined set of qualifying observations evaluated under a published Vault methodology. The term does not mean a financial or accounting audit.

EVIDENCE

Preserved information supporting what Vault observed, its source and timing, and the resulting historical record. Evidence does not imply an absolute guarantee that every external source or statement is error-free.

PUBLIC FREE PICK RECORD

A record of qualifying publicly observable free selections captured by Vault. It does not necessarily represent every selection, paid selection, private selection, or other activity of the consultant.

CLAIMED

The consultant has established control of the corresponding Vault profile. Claiming a profile does not erase or rewrite previously preserved observations.

Public observation standard

Vault may preserve qualifying information that is publicly accessible without joining a paid or private service. Information must satisfy the applicable Vault eligibility and timing standards before it contributes to a public observed record.

Vault distinguishes original evidence from later interpretation and grading. A correction to an interpretation or grade does not silently replace the original preserved evidence.

Tracked records describe what Vault qualifyingly observed. They should not be read as a representation of a consultant's complete historical activity unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Corrections and disputes

A consultant does not need to claim a profile to dispute an attribution, interpretation, grade, identity match, or other published information. Valid corrections are recorded without silently rewriting the original evidence trail.

Scope

This public methodology intentionally describes Vault's standards rather than its proprietary implementation. Collection architecture, source prioritization, identity-resolution methods, scoring, automation, anti-fraud controls, internal thresholds, and acquisition systems are confidential operational information.
